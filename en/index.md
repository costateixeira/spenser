### What it is

**Spenser is a chocolate dispenser with a FHIR interface: order a chocolate as a medication, and adjust and monitor what is in its lanes.**

This open source specification provides everything you need to build your own:

* Hardware (3D printable parts and electronic components)
* Software (for the ESP32 chip)

[Using Spenser](usage.md) covers what it is for and how to order from it. [Interfaces](interfaces.md) covers everything it speaks. Many units have been consumed to test the system's operation and effect.
