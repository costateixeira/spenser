ActorDefinition: Unit Owner

Official URL: http://costateixeira.github.io/spenser/ActorDefinition/UnitOwner Version: 1.0.0
Draft as of 2026-11-13 Computable Name: UnitOwner

The person who holds a unit's key, and with it the authority to configure, restock and update the unit.

Language: en

Actor: UnitOwner Unit OwnerType: person

Reading a unit is open, but changing one needs its key. A script acting with the key acts as the owner.

See What needs a key.
